The Problem

Every AI-first team hits the same wall

🔄

Knowledge resets every conversation

Your AI tools start from zero each session. Every developer rebuilds their own prompts. Decisions made in Monday's meeting are gone by Friday.

🏝️

AI silos everywhere

Claude knows something, GPT-4 knows something else, Cursor knows nothing. There's no shared layer — just scattered context that disappears.

❓

No truth layer

You can't tell if that doc is current, that decision is final, or that fact is just someone's best guess from 6 months ago.

The Solution

One shared brain for your entire team

xbrain is a cloud-hosted AI memory layer that sits between your team and your AI tools. Every conversation, document, and agent output lands in one place — tagged by team, by project, by truth level. Accessible from LibreChat, Open WebUI, your Chrome browser, and the API.

Features

Everything your team needs, already set up

No Docker. No VM. No maintenance. Just your team's shared brain.

🧠

Persistent Team Memory

Every conversation, web clip, and Drive document becomes a permanent, searchable memory item. Tagged automatically with your team scope and project.

📊

5 Truth Levels

Facts climb from EPHEMERAL → WORKING → VALIDATED → CANONICAL → PUBLIC. CANONICAL facts are automatically injected in every AI conversation for your team.

🤖

Multi-Model AI

Connect Claude, GPT-4, and Grok in one interface. Switch models mid-conversation. All outputs feed the same shared memory.

🔌

Built-in Integrations

Google Drive sync, Chrome web clipper, Calendar access, deck generator — all connected, all team-scoped, all logged in your audit trail.

🐙

GitHub Team Auth

Restrict access to your GitHub Organization members. Zero user management — if they're in your GitHub Org, they're in xbrain.

🕸️

Knowledge Graph

Entities, relationships, and temporal facts extracted automatically. Ask "who was responsible for X in Q3?" and get an answer.
