The problem

Your team runs agents.
They don't run together.

Every developer, marketer, and ops person on your team has their own AI — and their own isolated context bubble. The more agents you add, the more context you lose.

Agents start blind

Your dev agent builds something. Your marketing agent has never heard of it. Each new AI session resets to zero — no matter what your team already knows.

You're the middleware

Dev finishes. You copy context to marketing. Marketing finishes. You brief ops. You're spending your time being the connector between agents that should be connected already.

Adding agents adds chaos

More agents means more isolated context. The coordination overhead grows faster than the team. You added agents to move faster — but something's still missing.

The solution

Cortex OS is the cognitive layer
your agents share.

Every agent reads from the same brain. Every output feeds back into it. Your team moves as one — automatically.

Cognitive Core

The shared memory layer. Vector, graph, and relational storage for every decision, output, and fact your team produces. Scoped by team, searchable by all.

Agent Bus

MCP-native integration layer. Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT, Grok — any agent your team uses connects via the Agent Bus and instantly reads shared context.

Truth Engine

Five truth levels — Ephemeral to Canonical. Agents act on validated context, not drafts. Decisions get promoted automatically as your team validates them.

Graphiti
Temporal knowledge graph · under the hood

The brain that renews itself.

Most memory systems just accumulate — they add new facts on top of old ones until context becomes noise. Cortex OS uses Graphiti to build a temporal knowledge graph: when new information arrives, it automatically updates entity relationships, deprecates what's no longer true, and resolves contradictions. The brain stays current, not just larger.

Time-aware facts

When auth-v2 ships, Graphiti marks auth-v1 as superseded. Agents retrieve what's true now, not what was true six months ago.

Automatic contradiction resolution

A decision changes. Graphiti reconciles the conflict across the graph — no duplicate truths, no stale context quietly misleading your agents.

Entity & relationship tracking

Every concept your team produces — features, decisions, people, services — becomes a node in a live graph your agents can traverse and reason over.
