xbrain — Settings

Saved automatically. Settings sync across devices via your Chrome profile.

Show the xbrain UI in Chrome's side panel instead of a floating popup. Side panel stays open during the Google sign-in flow — no more "had to click Connect twice".

On chat.grooveos.app, when the API key dialog appears for the Claude Pro/Max endpoint, pre-fill it with your xbrain bridge token. No more copy-paste.

These settings live in chrome.storage.sync. The bridge token itself stays local-only (chrome.storage.local) and is never synced.
