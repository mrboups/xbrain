<objective>
Delete the paywall that Q6 cancelled but nobody removed.

`apps/memory-api/app/deps.py::require_paid_tier` raises **403 "CRM and task tracking require a Team or Enterprise plan"** whenever a team's `plan` column is `starter`. Migration `0008_team_plan.py` creates that column with `server_default="starter"`, and **no code path anywhere upgrades a team**. So on every fresh install — i.e. for every self-hoster the OSS product exists for — all 10 `/v1/crm/*` and `/v1/tasks/*` endpoints return 403, telling the operator to buy a plan that cannot be bought.

Locked decision **Q6**: *"Nothing in the product is paywalled. Monetize the hosted service only."* This gate is a survivor of the cancelled paid-tier design. Plan 15-02 correctly classified `crm` and `tasks` as CORE routers — but mounting a router that immediately 403s every caller is not shipping a feature.

It went unnoticed because xbrain's own production team was almost certainly promoted by hand in the database, so the people who would have hit it never did.
</objective>

<this_is_an_access_control_widening_treat_it_as_such>
You are turning a 403 into a 200. That is a **widening of access**, and it deserves more care than a feature addition, not less.

The dependency chain today is `require_paid_tier` → `get_team_scope` → `get_current_principal`. It stacks a *plan* check on top of an *authentication + membership* check. You are removing **only the plan check**. Authentication and team membership must remain exactly as strong as they are now.

The failure you must not cause: dropping `require_paid_tier` in a way that also drops `get_team_scope`, so `/v1/crm/*` stops enforcing team isolation and one team can read another's contacts. `team_scope` isolation is *the* differentiator of this product and the one invariant that must never break. Your test must prove it still holds.
</this_is_an_access_control_widening_treat_it_as_such>
