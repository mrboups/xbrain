| verify script → local Docker daemon | Scripts drive `docker compose`; the config-layer checks need no daemon (pure parse). |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
